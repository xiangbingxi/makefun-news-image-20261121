# The 36 stories — sources (v2)

Every line below was checked against reporting published on Saturday
21 November 2026 (or, where noted, first reported that day from an event
the day before). Figures are the figures the cited outlets reported;
where outlets differed, the page uses the figure most outlets agreed on
and hedges the rest with "says" or "reported". This is the v2 front
page: 36 stories, 7 regions, 3 big headlines, 10 small illustrated
stories, 23 numbered briefs.

## Big headlines

1. **RIYADH — Three missiles down over Riyadh.** Saudi defences
   intercepted three Houthi missiles aimed at the capital early on
   Saturday — the second attack on Riyadh in days. Eight emergency
   alerts in 24 hours. The first strike, on Thursday, hit the PIF
   tower; that is listed under DATE CHECK, not as a Saturday story.
   *Arab News, AP, Reuters.*
2. **POLAND — Russian missiles land in Poland.** Two Russian cruise
   missiles and a suspected jet-powered drone came down in south-eastern
   Poland during a major overnight Russian bombardment of Ukraine. A
   Kh-101 cruise missile fell near Nowy Lipowiec, about 50 km from the
   Ukrainian border; the other objects near Rudnik nad Sanem and
   Molodycz, about 35 km from the allied war games at Nowa Deba.
   Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it an "intentional provocation";
   NATO chief Mark Rutte called it a "grave violation" and scrambled
   jets. No injuries or damage reported in Poland. The Ukraine barrage
   killed 8 and injured about 28. *AP (via Manila Times), The Times,
   SCMP, Times of India, Moneycontrol.*
3. **MERCURY — BepiColombo reaches Mercury.** After eight years in
   space, the ESA/JAXA probe is captured by Mercury's gravity today.
   Its two orbiters separate in December; science begins in April 2027.
   Launched October 2018. *ESA, JAXA, AP.*
