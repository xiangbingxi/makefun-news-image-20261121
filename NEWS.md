# The 16 stories — sources

Every line below was checked against reporting published on Saturday
21 November 2026 (or, where noted, first reported that day from an event
the day before). Figures are the figures the cited outlets reported;
where outlets differed, the page uses the figure most outlets agreed on
and hedges the rest with "says" or "reported".

## Featured

1. **POLAND — Russian missiles land in Poland.** Two Russian cruise
   missiles and a suspected jet-powered drone came down in south-eastern
   Poland during a major overnight Russian bombardment of Ukraine. A
   Kh-101 cruise missile fell near Nowy Lipowiec, about 50 km from the
   Ukrainian border; the other objects near Rudnik nad Sanem and
   Molodycz, about 35 km from the allied war games at Nowa Deba.
   Prime Minister Donald Tusk called it an "intentional provocation";
   NATO chief Mark Rutte called it a "grave violation" and scrambled
   jets. No injuries or damage reported in Poland. The Ukraine barrage
   killed 8 and injured about 28. *AP (via Manila Times), The Times,
   SCMP, Times of India, Moneycontrol.*
2. **SCIENCE — Claude "proves" the Riemann hypothesis.** Anthropic
   mathematician Levent Alpoge said on Friday that he and Claude had
   proved the Riemann hypothesis, first posed in 1859 and the most
   famous open problem in mathematics, releasing a Lean formalisation.
   The claim was not independently verified at announcement; by Saturday
   independent mathematicians, including MIT's Andrew Sutherland, had
   compiled the Lean code and called it "almost certainly true". A
   second, human proof by Alessandro Masullo was also posted the same
   day. *Phys.org, Scientific American, WSJ, DataCamp.*
3. **MARKETS — Tech sell-off deepens.** The S&P 500 fell 3.7% and the
   Nasdaq turned negative for the year as the AI sell-off deepened;
   Anthropic shares slid days after a record IPO. *Jabulani Radio
   (Nov 21), Viral SA Metro roundup.*
4. **CLIMATE — COP31 ends in deadlock.** The UN climate talks in
   Antalya, Turkiye, reached their final scheduled day with no
   fossil-fuel phase-out in the draft cover decision and no agreement in
   sight; talks spilled into overtime. Ukraine blocked a Russian climate
   report that used data from occupied Ukrainian territory.
   *AP (via Bastille Post), Reuters, Ukrinform.*
5. **DENMARK — Russia accused of assassination plot.** Denmark accused
   Russian intelligence of plotting to kill a US-Ukrainian rapper on
   Danish soil and expelled a Russian diplomat. *AP (via Manila Times),
   Viral SA Metro roundup.*
6. **USA — 19 1/2 years for Sikh murder plot.** A US court sentenced
   Indian national Nikhil Gupta to 235 months (19 1/2 years) for a
   murder-for-hire plot against a Sikh separatist leader in New York.
   *The Hindu (Morning Digest, 21 Nov), AP (via Manila Times).*
